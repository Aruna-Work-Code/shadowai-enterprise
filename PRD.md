# 1. Product Overview

ShadowAI is an AI Governance Decision Assistant designed to help organizations adopt AI without losing visibility, policy control, or accountability.

The product addresses two connected governance problems:

1. Employees need safe and approved AI capabilities to complete their work.
2. Organizations need to discover and resolve AI usage that happens outside the approved AI inventory.

ShadowAI therefore combines:

### Preventive Governance

**Safe Alternative Recommendation**

Help employees find an approved AI capability before they use an unsafe or unmanaged alternative.

### Corrective Governance

**Governance Resolution Intelligence**

Discover unmanaged AI usage, classify risk, create investigations, support governance decisions, and maintain an auditable record.

The product principle is:

> **AI governance should not only block risky behavior. It should help organizations make better, faster, safer, and more accountable decisions about AI.**

# 2. Problem Statement

Enterprise AI adoption creates a difficult balance.

Employees increasingly use AI to:

- write and summarize information
- analyze data
- generate code
- research topics
- automate repetitive work
- improve productivity

However, organizations may not know:

- which AI tools employees are using
- whether those tools are approved
- what type of data is being shared
- who owns the tool
- how risky the usage is
- whether an exception is legitimate
- what action should be taken
- whether governance decisions are properly documented
